# Contributors

This file acknowledges everyone who has contributed to pyvoa, in any form:
code, data sources, bug reports, documentation, teaching material, translations,
design and testing.

It is deliberately broader than `AUTHORS`, which lists the **authors of the
software** — the people who have made a substantial and sustained intellectual
contribution to its design, implementation or scientific direction (see
[CONTRIBUTING.md §8](CONTRIBUTING.md#8-licence-and-authorship)). Being listed
here carries no obligation and no authorship claim; it is a thank-you.

If you have contributed and are not listed, that is an oversight: please open a
pull request adding yourself, or write to <contact@pyvoa.org>. Order is
alphabetical by family name and implies no hierarchy.
