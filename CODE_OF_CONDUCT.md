# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

pyvoa is a scientific and educational project. It is used by high-school and
university students, teachers, journalists and researchers, many of whom are
newcomers to programming or to open source. Our community must therefore be a
place where asking a basic question is safe.

We commit to making participation in pyvoa a respectful experience for everyone,
whatever their age, background, disability or neurodiversity, sex or gender,
gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, physical characteristics,
language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic
position, or level of education and experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and prefer a question to an accusation.
- Give and receive criticism about ideas, code and data — never about persons.
- Be patient with beginners: explaining is part of the purpose of this project.
- Respect the working languages of the project (English for code, issues and
  documentation; French welcome in discussions) and the effort made by
  non-native speakers.
- Credit the work of others accurately, including data providers and prior
  projects.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual or romantic attention.
- Demeaning, insulting or discriminatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit
  permission.
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above.

Disagreement is not misconduct. Scientific and technical disputes are expected;
they are settled with arguments and evidence, in public, and without hostility.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — the GitHub repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, the project contact address, the website — and whenever
someone is officially representing pyvoa in public, online or offline, including
at conferences, hackathons and teaching events.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the maintainers at **<contact@pyvoa.org>**.

If your report concerns a maintainer, you may instead contact any other
maintainer individually; their institutional addresses are listed in `AUTHORS`.

Reports are handled confidentially and as promptly as possible. You will receive
an acknowledgement of receipt, and you will be informed of the outcome unless
doing so would compromise someone's safety or privacy. People who report in good
faith will not face retaliation, and a maintainer concerned by a report takes no
part in its handling.

## Addressing and repairing harm

The maintainers determine the consequences of any violation, in proportion to
its severity and, wherever possible, with the aim of repairing harm and allowing
the person to return to the community. Measures may include:

1. **Private conversation** — a private, written clarification of what was
   inappropriate, possibly with a request for a public or private apology.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning, with a defined period of no interaction with
   the people involved.
3. **Temporary suspension** — temporary exclusion from project spaces.
4. **Permanent exclusion** — for sustained, severe or repeated violations, in
   particular harassment of an individual or aggression towards a group.

Maintainers who do not enforce this Code in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions decided by the other maintainers.
